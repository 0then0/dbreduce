# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Treat contributors and users respectfully, regardless of background, identity,
experience or technical opinion. Give specific, constructive feedback, accept
corrections, and discuss the work rather than attacking the person.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks,
and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces,
including communication made while representing DBReduce.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project maintainer is [@0then0](https://github.com/0then0). Use a private
contact method listed on that profile to report a concern. If no private method
is available, open an issue requesting private contact without identifying the
people involved or publishing incident details.

Reports should describe what happened and provide relevant context. The maintainer
will handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to investigate
and respond. Do not post another person's private information in a public issue.

Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a request to change
behavior, removal of content, a warning, or temporary or permanent restrictions
on participation. Decisions should explain the behavior and applicable rule.
